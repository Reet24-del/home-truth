# Agreement for Sale (draft): Apartment A-704, Nimbus Greens Phase 1

This Agreement for Sale is made between Kestrel Habitat Pvt. Ltd. (the
"Promoter") and the person named in Schedule 1 (the "Allottee").

## 1. The apartment

The Promoter agrees to sell, and the Allottee agrees to buy, Apartment A-704 on
the 7th floor of Building A in Nimbus Greens Phase 1 (registration number
DEMO-RERA-0001), having a carpet area of 64.10 sq m (690 sq ft).

## 2. Consideration

| Item                                        | Amount       |
| ------------------------------------------- | ------------ |
| Price of the apartment                      | ₹72,00,000   |
| Covered car parking space (1 no.)           | ₹4,50,000    |
| GST at 5% on the price of the apartment     | ₹3,60,000    |
| Maintenance deposit (24 months)             | ₹1,20,000    |
| Legal and documentation charges             | ₹25,000      |
| **Total payable**                           | **₹81,55,000** |

Total payable: ₹81,55,000, exclusive of stamp duty and registration charges,
which shall be borne by the Allottee.

## 3. Possession

The Promoter shall hand over possession of the apartment on or before
31 December 2028, subject to clause 5.

## 4. Delay in possession

If the Promoter fails to hand over possession by the date in clause 3, the
Promoter shall pay the Allottee compensation at ₹5 per sq ft of carpet area for
every month of delay, and this shall be the Allottee's sole remedy.

## 5. Force majeure

The Promoter shall not be liable for delay caused by force majeure, which
includes war, flood, fire, earthquake or other natural calamity, and also
includes delay in obtaining any approval from any government authority, and
shortage of labour or construction material.

## 6. Payments

All payments shall be made by cheque or bank transfer to the Nimbus Greens
Phase 1 RERA Designated Account.

## 7. Common areas and facilities

The Allottee shall have the right to use the common areas and facilities listed
in Annexure B: clubhouse with gymnasium, jogging track, children's play area and
landscaped garden.

## 8. Defect liability

If any structural defect or defect in workmanship is brought to the notice of
the Promoter within a period of one year from the date of handing over
possession, the Promoter shall rectify it without further charge.

## 9. Cancellation by the Allottee

If the Allottee cancels this agreement, the Promoter may forfeit 20% of the
price of the apartment and refund the balance without interest within twelve
months.
